About the counselling approach

I provide a person-centred approach to counselling.

Approach
The person-centred approach to counselling is a humanistic therapy founded by Carl Rogers, an American psychologist.
Empowerment
Person-centred therapy harnesses people’s natural self-healing process and empowers you to take control of your mental health.
Tools
It provides you with the tools and resources to understand yourself and what you need to achieve positive change in your life.
Commitment
I am very passionate about this approach and I believe that the nurturing and trusting relationship between the client and therapist is key to any therapy.

What services does Rosie Ilieva offer?

As a person-centred practitioner I operate under the humanistic belief that every client has the capacity to change and grow given the right conditions and support.

This involves progressing the work at a pace that is tailored to your individual needs, prioritising collaboration, transparency and curiosity.

We will be working in partnership to build trust and create a therapeutic and safe environment in which change and growth can happen.

What would the first session look like?

For all new clients, I offer an initial, free of charge 30-minute telephone or online consultation to discuss aims for therapy.

This will help me understand what is bringing you to therapy and if I may be able to help. It also provides an opportunity for you to ask any questions and aims to help you decide if you feel working with me will be a good fit for your needs.

All communications will be treated in complete confidence.
